# Job Description Analysis — India Software Roles

**Research date:** 24 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Compare software-development responsibilities and technical requirements with the recorded B.Tech CSE curriculum and identify potential areas for practical improvement.

## 1. Selection criteria

The sample prioritises roles advertised in India and uses official employer career pages. It includes a 2027-graduate internship, an early-career software engineering role, and a software-development role with more experience required.

These postings are not equally suitable for a 2027 graduate. They are used to identify technical skills and responsibilities, not to claim that every role is currently open or that the student meets every eligibility requirement.

## 4. Implications for curriculum recommendations

1. Retain programming, OOP, data structures and algorithms as core foundations.
2. Review web-development and database laboratories for application integration, validation, error handling and secure data access.
3. Review software-engineering assessments for test cases, debugging evidence, version control, code review and documentation.
4. Provide a small integrated application project where students can demonstrate programming, database interaction, testing and access control.
5. Review cloud and scalability practical work without assuming that every entry-level role requires advanced cloud expertise.
6. Do not remove or reduce course credits based only on this job sample. Confirm the applicable curriculum, syllabus, programme requirements and existing coverage first.

## 5. Limitations

- The sample contains three selected employer postings and does not represent the entire Indian software job market.
- The Amazon internship explicitly targets 2027 graduates. The Cisco role has an early-career experience range but its application status and publication date could not be verified from the accessible page. The Wipro role requires 3–5 years of experience and is included only as a technical comparator.
- Job postings can close or change. The links should be checked again before any application or final submission.
- The comparison uses advertised requirements and responsibilities, not interviews with recruiters or employers.
- Course mappings are based on transcript titles and should be verified against the applicable official curriculum and detailed syllabi.

**Conclusion:** The postings support retaining strong programming and problem-solving foundations while reviewing practical coverage of testing, debugging, integration and documentation. They do not, by themselves, justify a credit reduction or prove that any existing course is inadequate.
