Time-Saving Scenario Calculator

Enter your assumptions to estimate the net monthly time saved by reducing follow-ups. This is a scenario estimate, not measured evidence.

Example using the default assumptions

Gross time saved: 50 minutes/month.

Net result: 10 minutes/month of additional time spent.

Formula:
Gross time saved = Follow-ups × Minutes per follow-up × (Reduction percentage ÷ 100)
Net time saved = Gross time saved − Dashboard support time

This estimate excludes initial development time and other costs or benefits. Replace the assumptions with measured values before using the result to make a real decision.
